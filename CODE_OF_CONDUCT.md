# 社区行为准则 / Code of Conduct

我们致力于建设一个开放、友善、尊重差异的协作社区。参与本项目即表示你同意：

- 尊重不同经验、观点、身份与背景的参与者。
- 反馈聚焦于代码、设计与行为，不进行人身攻击。
- 不发布骚扰、歧视、威胁、色情或泄露他人隐私的内容。
- 接受维护者为保护社区而进行的合理管理。

如遇不当行为，请通过 GitHub 私密安全报告联系维护者，并提供必要上下文。维护者可删除不当内容、限制参与或采取其他适当措施。

---

We are committed to an open, welcoming, and respectful community. By participating, you agree to respect people of different backgrounds and experience levels, keep feedback focused on the work, avoid harassment or disclosure of private information, and follow reasonable maintainer moderation.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub's security reporting flow with the relevant context. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action to protect the community.
